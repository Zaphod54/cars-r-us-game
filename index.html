How to Play

Cars R Us

Drive forever through three lanes of traffic. Dodge every car you can, grab helpful powerups, and keep your 3 lives for as long as possible.

Powerups

Shield

Blocks one crash.

Extra Life

Adds one life.

Turbo

Scores faster.

Clear Road

Removes cars ahead.

Negative Powerups

Puddle

Slows your car.

Shield Breaker

Removes your shield.

Oil Slick

Jams steering.

Controls

Use A or Left Arrow to move left one lane.

Use D or Right Arrow to move right one lane.

On touch screens, tap the left and right buttons.

How You Die

You start with 3 lives. Each car crash takes away 1 life unless a shield saves you.

When your lives reach 0, the game is over. You can also quit anytime.
